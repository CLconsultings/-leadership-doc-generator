# Deck validation checklist

Run this before delivering any Leadership-Link deck. Each item is a check with a
specific failure mode. When an item fails, revise before delivery rather than shipping
the deck with a caveat.

## Content and narrative

- **Narrative sequence.** The deck moves through context or tension, why it matters,
  framework or concept, practical application, participant or leader action, and
  reinforcement or next step. It is a presentation, not a document split into pages.
- **Framework fidelity.** Every framework name, sequence, dimension, and definition
  matches the approved source exactly. Nothing was invented, renamed, merged, or
  simplified. Inferred detail is marked, not disguised.
- **Source accuracy.** Claims trace to approved materials. Fact, conclusion, inference,
  and speculation are distinguishable. No fabricated quotations, statistics, or client
  examples.
- **One job per slide.** Each slide has a single primary message. Slides doing two jobs
  are split.

## Visual and typographic

- **Visual hierarchy** and **readability** hold at presentation distance.
- **Spacing** and **content density** leave real whitespace; no text walls.
- **Logo treatment** uses the approved asset and capitalization; nothing recreated from
  memory.
- **Typography** uses the approved fonts and sentence case.
- **Minimum sizing.** Body content is at least 18 pt. The 11 pt exception appears only
  on muted, non-substantive structural labels, never on body copy, instructions,
  evidence, model definitions, or participant actions.
- **Slide numbering** is present and correct where required.

## Production integrity

- **Speaker-note completeness.** Notes add value beyond the slide and do not repeat it
  verbatim.
- **Editable object integrity.** Text and shapes are native editable objects; no
  complete slide was flattened into an image. Masters and layouts are consistent.
- **Export quality.** The requested native and presentation formats render correctly,
  and the editable PowerPoint remains the primary source file.

## Governance

- **Confidentiality.** No cross-client information leaked; no internal corrections or
  production notes exposed in client-facing output.
- **Corrections Log applied.** Past failures recorded in
  `corrections-log.md` were checked and prevented in this deck, and the log itself is
  not exposed in client-facing work.
- **Release standard met.** The deck satisfies the "What Great Looks Like" criteria in
  `corrections-log.md`.
