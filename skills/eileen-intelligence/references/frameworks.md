## Managers in Motion

Approved sequence, in order:

1. Know Yourself and Others
2. Motivate Your People and Manage the Work
3. Give Feedback That Drives Performance and Growth
4. Manage Up and Out

Treat the application outputs below as application deliverables supported by the
reinforcement materials. Do not describe them as the complete formal curriculum unless
the approved facilitator guide or source deck confirms that wording.

### Block 1: Know Yourself and Others

Focus:

- transition from individual contributor to people manager
- self-awareness
- reading behavioral cues
- DiSC-informed adaptation
- working effectively with one's manager

Supportable application outputs:

- identify a situation requiring a people-manager mindset
- observe tone, pace, word choice, and likely behavioral style
- plan an adaptation for a manager or team interaction
- test the adaptation and reflect on its impact

Do not replace the canonical title with "Becoming a Great People Manager" unless an
approved source specifically requires that alternate heading.

### Block 2: Motivate Your People and Manage the Work

Focus:

- priority clarity
- line of sight
- individual motivation
- behavioral style
- managing distractions
- protecting the most important work

Supportable application outputs:

- identify where a team member needs clearer priorities or alignment
- connect an individual task to departmental priorities
- identify a meaningful motivator for one team member
- distinguish major priorities from distractions
- plan one redirect

### Block 3: Give Feedback That Drives Performance and Growth

Focus:

- recognizing feedback opportunities
- reinforcing effective behavior
- addressing performance or growth needs
- creating a consistent feedback habit

Supportable application outputs:

- identify a missed feedback opportunity
- prepare positive feedback using Intent-Observation-Impact
- draft feedback using IOIC+A
- identify where feedback can become a recurring management practice
- prepare for or conduct an IOIC+A conversation

### Block 4: Manage Up and Out

Focus:

- communicating upward
- communicating across the organization
- adapting message content and delivery
- managerial presence
- continued application after the program

Supportable application outputs:

- identify an upward or outward message that must be delivered
- adjust the content or delivery of a manager update
- assess how the team interprets the manager's behavior during decisions or change
- select one program behavior to strengthen during the next month
